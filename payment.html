The ultimate live elimination battle. Last player standing wins the cash prize! 🏆

📥 STEP 1: JOIN THE LOBBY
  • Choose your entry tier (₦100 or ₦1,000) and pay securely via Paystack.
  • Once paid, you’ll be granted instant access to the Live Elimination Arena.
⚔️ STEP 2: BATTLE & SURVIVE
  • Tap to Attack: Click the "REMOVE" button to instantly knock a rival player out of the group.

🏆 STEP 3: WIN THE PRIZE
  • Keep knocking out rivals until the Players Remaining counter hits 1.
  • If you are the last survivor standing, you win the cash prize (₦50,000 for ₦100 tier / ₦500,000 for ₦1,000 tier)!
